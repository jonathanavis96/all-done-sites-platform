The short version

This is your reference for making changes to the Baobab Wines website yourselves, without needing to ask anyone to edit code. Jump to any section using the tabs above or the dots on the left, or just scroll straight through, everything is on this one page.

  • The editor: baobabwines.com/admin, sign in with your own account
  • What you can edit: Producers, Wines, and the Home Page
  • The one thing to remember: a save takes about five minutes to appear live, it isn't instant

01 What you can edit

You each have your own login to a content editor for baobabwines.com. It lets you open the pages that already exist and change the words, photos and a few other details: no coding, no developer needed.

You can edit:

  • Producers: the story, photos and details for each estate you carry
  • Wines: tasting notes, bottle photos, awards and download links for each wine
  • Home Page: the hero banner, the featured wine of the month, the South Africa introduction, the core values, and the trade testimonials

You can't add a brand new producer or a brand new wine yourselves. That's a deliberate choice, not a limitation. Just email me at jonathan@alldonesites.com and I'll add it. You can edit every existing one freely.

02 Logging in

Open the editor →
  1. Go to baobabwines.com/admin.
  2. Sign in with your own account, Samantha and Giles each have one.
  3. You'll see a list on the left: Producers, Wines and Home Page. Click any of them to see the entries inside.
  4. Further down you'll also see Media Manager, that's covered in section 5, and a Cloud group (Project Config, User Management, Support). Leave the Cloud group alone; it's account administration, not page content, and isn't something you need for day-to-day editing.

03 Saves take five minutes

This is the single most important thing to know.

⏱ A save is not instant

When you click Save, your change does not appear on the live site immediately. It goes through a short automatic process in the background, and it normally takes about five minutes to appear on baobabwines.com.

So: make your change, click Save, and give it five minutes before you go looking for it. If you refresh the site straight away and it looks unchanged, that's expected, it isn't broken, it just hasn't finished yet.

04 Editing each section

Producers

Open Producers and pick one, for example Opstal. The fields you'll use most:

FieldWhat it does
Producer Name, Region, AppellationBasic details
Show this producer on the siteUntick to take a producer off the site entirely without losing anything, in case one ever needs to be paused
Tagline / Sales SentenceThe short lines shown near the top of the page
Hero Image (primary) / Hero Image GalleryThe main photo and the rotating photo set at the top of the page
Producer LogoThe round logo badge on the photo
Producer StoryThe main paragraphs about the estate. This is the actual text of the page: if you clear it, the story section disappears from the live site, so treat it as replace, don't delete
Sustainability / Vineyard & Cellar PracticeOptional extra sections that appear under the story only when you've written something in them
Featured quote / Featured PersonThe pull-quote and the name credited beneath it
Range SpotlightAn optional highlighted band further down the page (a heading, some text, a button, and photos)
AttributesThe small tags shown on the producer's card, e.g. Organic, Vegan, Old Vine
Producer videoPaste just the YouTube ID (the part after v= in the link) to change the film that plays near the bottom of the page

A typical edit: open Opstal, change the Sales Sentence, click Save, and check back in five minutes.

One thing worth knowing: the order the producer cards appear in on the /producers page is set by hand, not by anything you change here. If a card itself doesn't look different after an edit, that's usually why: Tagline and Region changes show up on the producer's own page, not on its card.

Wines

Open Wines and pick one. The fields you'll use most:

  • Wine Name, Producer (linked to the producer entry), Variety / Blend, Vintage, Style
  • Bottle Shot and Back Label Image
  • Tasting Notes: Nose, Palate, Food Pairing
  • Shelf-Talker Keywords: the short punchy list of 4 to 5 words shown on the wine page (e.g. "Cling Peach • Apple • Beeswax")
  • Awards: add an award body, score/medal and year; you can list several
  • Download Files: the web addresses for the tasting note, shelf talker and tech sheet PDFs
  • Additional Notes: free text at the bottom of the page

Home Page

Open Home Page, there's only one entry. It's split into sections:

  • Hero section: the big headline and subtext at the very top, plus the two buttons beneath it
  • Featured Wine of the Month: pick which wine is featured, an optional second wine to rotate with it, the headline above it, and an optional story override
  • South Africa intro section: the heading, two paragraphs, and the three stat blocks (e.g. number of producers, hectares under vine)
  • Core Values section: the numbered statements further down the page
  • Testimonials: the trade partner quotes shown on the homepage, inside this same Home Page entry (there isn't a separate Testimonials section in the sidebar). Each one has a Quote, Attribution (name), Company / restaurant, an optional Review logo / avatar, an optional Star rating, and a Publish on homepage tick box, untick it to hold a quote back without deleting it, for example while you're waiting on written permission to use it

05 Formatting text and adding images

Bold and italic. In the larger text boxes (Producer Story, Additional Notes), select some text and use the bold or italic buttons that appear above it, the same way you would in Word or Google Docs.

Images. Click any image box and a picker opens. You can choose an existing photo already on the site, or upload a new one from your computer. Once picked, click Save as normal.

Media Manager. There's also a standalone Media Manager in the left menu, below Producers/Wines/Home Page. It's the same photo library the image pickers use, open it if you just want to upload a batch of photos ahead of time, or browse what's already there, without editing a page at the same time.

A quick word on the Cloud group further down (Project Config, User Management, Support): that's account administration, not page content, leave it alone. It isn't part of editing the site.

06 If something looks wrong

  1. Wait five minutes first, see section 03. Most "it's not working" moments are just the save still going through.
  2. If it still looks wrong after that, email me at jonathan@alldonesites.com and tell me which page and which field. A screenshot really helps, so attach one if you can.

If a change still isn't showing

Browsers cache both the editor and the live site quite aggressively. So even after waiting the five minutes, a normal refresh can still show you the old version, because your browser is showing you a saved copy instead of fetching the current page.

The fix is a hard refresh, which forces the browser to fetch everything fresh:

🔄 Hard refresh
Windows
Ctrl + Shift + R
Mac, Chrome or Firefox
Cmd + Shift + R
Mac, Safari
Cmd + Option + R

Safari is the odd one out on purpose: there, Cmd + Shift + R switches on Reader view instead of reloading, so use Cmd + Option + R.

This applies both to the live site and to the editor itself, if the editor is showing you something odd, a hard refresh there is worth trying too.

07 Known limits: what you can't do here

  • You can't create a brand new producer or wine, or delete an existing one. Email me at jonathan@alldonesites.com and I'll add it.
  • A few fields you may remember from an earlier look have been removed because they weren't actually connected to anything on the live site. Nothing was lost by removing them, they simply didn't do what they looked like they did.
  • Blog posts and the About page aren't editable here.
  • Some details (like technical specs used in trade documents) aren't shown on the public pages even though they're stored, that's by design, not a bug.
